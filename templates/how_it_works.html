How to Use PowerSolution

Follow these simple steps to calculate your electricity requirements and estimate the inverter, batteries and solar panels needed for your home or business.

Open Energy Calculator

Calculate Your Power Needs in 6 Easy Steps

You don't need to be an electrical engineer to get started.

01

Select Your Appliances

Open the calculator and choose the electrical appliances you use, such as LED lights, televisions, fans, refrigerators and air conditioners.

Add a separate row for each appliance type.

02

Enter Quantity and Daily Usage

Enter the number of appliances, their power ratings in watts and how many hours you use them each day.

Example: 5 LED lights × 10 watts × 6 hours = 300 Wh (0.3 kWh) per day.
03

Configure Your Power System

Choose your preferred battery chemistry, system voltage and backup duration.

The calculator also provides technical settings such as power factor, inverter margin, depth of discharge and peak sun hours. You can retain the suggested defaults if you are unsure.

04

Click Calculate My System

After entering your details, click the Calculate My System button.

PowerSolution estimates your connected load, daily energy usage, inverter capacity, battery requirements and solar array size.

05

Review Equipment and Estimated Costs

Review the recommended inverter, batteries and solar panels, including quantities, specifications and estimated prices.

Read any warnings or recommendations displayed in your calculation results.

06

Generate Your Quotation

Enter your name, email address and phone number in the quotation section.

Generate your quotation and download the PDF for your records or to discuss with a solar installation professional.

Understanding Your Calculation Results

Here is what the main figures mean.

⚡ Connected Load (W)

The total power rating of your selected appliances, assuming they operate together.

📊 Daily Energy (kWh)

The estimated amount of electricity your appliances consume each day.

🔌 Inverter Capacity (kVA)

The estimated inverter capacity needed to support your connected electrical load.

🔋 Battery Requirements

The estimated battery storage needed for your selected backup duration.

☀️ Solar Array (W)

The estimated solar panel capacity based on your daily energy consumption, sunlight hours and system losses.

💰 Estimated Cost

The estimated cost of recommended equipment using prices available in the application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the PowerSolution calculator free to use?

You can use the calculator to estimate your electricity requirements. Any applicable quotation or payment terms will be shown in the application.

What if I don't know an appliance's wattage?

Check the rating label or manufacturer specifications. PowerSolution may provide a default wattage, which you can adjust to match your actual appliance.

Are the recommended equipment sizes exact?

No. Results are estimates. A qualified professional should confirm starting loads, battery compatibility, installation conditions and final equipment selection.

Can I download my quotation?

Yes. Once you have successfully generated a quotation, you can download the PDF using the quotation feature.
